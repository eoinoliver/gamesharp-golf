How this number moves ▾

Every reviewed round compares the strokes your decisions donated against what's expected at your rating. Beat the expectation and DR rises; donate more and it falls. Daily tour calls nudge it. Nothing else touches it — not luck, not putts lipping out, not your ball-striking. Judgment only.
